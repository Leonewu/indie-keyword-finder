# Trend Discovery Product

An independently branded, public open-source product for keyword trend research and early rising-query discovery.

Its product promise is: 从一个种子词出发，尽早发现正在上升、值得进一步验证的搜索机会。

The selected first-release interface is Centered Focus: Mining opens first, the seed action is centered, navigation stays compact at the top, and rising-query results follow in a calm vertical list without a permanent left rail.

## Language

**Product Brand**:
Indie Keyword Finder is the selected independent public brand. `Keyword Finder` carries the searched SEO category language; `Indie` distinguishes the product and signals its independent-site audience.
_Avoid_: abandoned candidate names, an unmodified Keyword Finder title, coined names that hide the category, all-in-one SEO suite, legacy-product successor framing, ranking tool, search-volume tool, crypto miner

**Extension**:
The Chrome extension used by independent-site operators, SEO practitioners, and content creators to batch-research keywords and discover rising related queries through Google Trends.
_Avoid_: clone framing, hotspot browser

**Mining**:
A recursive discovery process that expands seed keywords through Google Trends related queries and qualifies candidates using relative time-series signals.
_Avoid_: Search-volume lookup, web crawler

**Seed Keyword**:
The user-provided starting query from which Mining expands related queries.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed opportunity, exact-demand keyword

**Search Opportunity**:
A related query showing an early upward relative signal that is worth further human validation. It is not a guaranteed demand level, ranking outcome, or exact search volume.
_Avoid_: New word, winning keyword, guaranteed trend

**Companion Skill**:
The independently executable, agent-facing mining workflow distributed in the same public repository as the Extension. It accepts research parameters and produces keyword-discovery results without requiring the Extension.
_Avoid_: Plugin
